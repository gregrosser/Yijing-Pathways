The Inversion Spine

A vertical column of 64 rows. Row n pairs {zōng(n), n} — n counts sequentially 1 to 64 on the right, and its inversion (line order reversed top-to-bottom) sits on the left, each shown as a circled hexagram number, the two set close together with no separating symbol. The 8 self-inverse hexagrams (zōng(n) = n) are highlighted; every other row's partner reappears later in the spine at its own row, with the pair reversed.
