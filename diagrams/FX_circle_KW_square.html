FX Circle ↔ KW Square

The FuXi (FX) binary sequence and the King Wen (KW) traditional sequence number the same 64 hexagrams two different ways, but the underlying relational groups — quartets and pairs of mutually inverse hexagrams — are identical either way. Click any hexagram, in either diagram, to see the equivalence: FX{3,4,63,64} == KW{23,24,43,44}.
