KW Square

The 64 hexagrams in traditional King Wen order, arranged 8×8. Following the Legge orientation, KW{1} sits at the top right, with each row descending right-to-left: row 1 is KW{8..1}, row 2 is KW{16..9}, and so on down to KW{64} at bottom left.
